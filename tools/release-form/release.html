About the NYSVMS mediation service

The NYSVMS mediation service helps coordinate a resolution to a concern you may have with a veterinarian who is a member of NYSVMS. You should first try to discuss your concerns with your veterinarian, but sometimes a third party is needed to help foster a resolution. We do not take disciplinary action; that is handled by the NY State Office of the Professions. We do not negotiate financial settlements. Many clients have found it valuable to talk with us about what happened, and in nearly all the cases we handle we are able to address the concerns brought to us.
