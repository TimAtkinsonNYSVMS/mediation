Mediation for pet owners

Help understanding the care your animal received

NYSVMS volunteer veterinarians help pet owners understand the treatment provided by a veterinarian who is a member of the Society, and help communicate your concerns to them.

Start a request Check on a request
About 10 minutes to fill inA volunteer is usually assigned within two weeks

Please know before you start

We have no connection to the State licensing board and cannot impose penalties or remove licenses.

We can only help when the veterinarian is a member of NYSVMS.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what happened

    Answer a few questions about the visit and your veterinarian. You can attach records and photos.

  2. 2

    A volunteer is assigned

    A volunteer veterinarian, your facilitator, is usually assigned within two weeks and contacts you directly by email or phone.

  3. 3

    They talk with your vet

    Your facilitator helps you understand the care and shares your concerns with the veterinarian. You can sign in any time to see where things stand.

Is mediation right for you?

If your concern is about fees or discipline, another organization can help you faster.
