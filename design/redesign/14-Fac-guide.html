How mediation works

Four steps from assignment to resolution.

  1. 1

    Accept the assignment

    Staff ask you to take a mediation request. You get an email and it appears under My assignments.

    Go to My assignments
  2. 2

    Introduce yourself

    Introduce yourself to the owner, then to the veterinarian, using Draft a letter on the request page.

  3. 3

    Log every contact

    Log each call or email as an update so staff can see where things stand, and keep the status current.

  4. 4

    Close the request

    When it's resolved, choose Close request: record the resolution, which sets the status to Completed. Then send the two resolution letters. The owner's letter includes a feedback link.
